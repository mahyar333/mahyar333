### About me

I am a PhD candidate at UCF, advised by Prof. George Atia, working in the Data Science Laboratory.
My research makes reinforcement learning agents better at **long-horizon tasks whose rewards depend on history**.
I do this by learning and using **automata (DFAs / reward machines)** that capture the task structure.

- **Automaton learning:** passive and online DFA inference (RPNI, L\*) from agent experience
- **Reward shaping and planning** over product MDPs with formal temporal constraints
- **Transfer learning:** distilling knowledge from abstract models and automata into deep RL agents
- **Neuro-symbolic RL with LLMs:** combining language-model reasoning with symbolic task structure
- **Applied work** with **DARPA** (transfer learning for autonomous decision-making) and **Siemens Energy** (AI for smart grids)
